How do I get more Google reviews as a tradie?

+

Ask every customer, the same day, with a direct link. We set you up with a QR card for the van and a ready-to-send text, and run a review catch-up for everyone you've worked for in the last 45 days. We never use fake or paid reviews.

Do I need a new website to show up on Google?

+

Usually not. For local searches, your Google Business Profile and reviews matter most. Your free business check will tell you if your website is broken, missing or slow, and only then do we suggest a new one.

Will my business show up in ChatGPT and other AI answers?

+

AI tools draw on Google Business Profiles, reviews, directory listings and websites. We make sure yours are complete, consistent and easy for them to read, which gives you the best chance. No one can honestly guarantee an AI mention or a top Google spot.

How is pindrop different from lead sites?

+

Lead sites charge per lead and often send the same job to several tradies. pindrop sets you up once, in your name, so customers ring you directly and you keep everything if you stop.

Do you need my passwords or customer list?

No. You add us as a manager on your Google profile, and you stay the owner. For the review catch-up, we write the texts and you send them from your own phone or job software.
